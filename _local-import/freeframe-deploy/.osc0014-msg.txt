OSC 0014: share-link downloads hand over the uploaded master (client delivery)

Sam 10/4: the client's share link + download of the approved version
without slate should happen on our own Review site. Upstream serves the MP4
remuxed from the top HLS rung on every download; for a delivery that is a
re-encode at web quality. A share-link download now serves the uploaded
file (the delivery master), named after the asset. OSC_SHARE_DOWNLOAD=mp4
restores upstream. Downloads stay off unless the link allows them, and each
one is logged as `downloaded` in the link's activity.
