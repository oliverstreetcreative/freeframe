fix(share): replies on share links were silently dropped (#439)

Frontend
- share-review-screen: onSubmitReply goes through submitComment with
  parent_id, then awaits refetchComments (the optimistic append only shows
  top-level rows). It is passed only when the link can comment.
- A guest with no identity yet is prompted first. The reply waits in its box
  on a promise that settles after the name is given (or quietly on cancel).
- comment-panel: Reply is offered only when there is a submit handler.
  InlineReplyInput keeps the text and shows the error instead of swallowing it.
- projects/[id]: the sidebar's dead reply box now posts via
  useComments().createComment(..., parentId).

Server
- guest_comment: the parent must be a live, non-internal comment on the
  link's asset (asset.id, not body.asset_id). Anything else is now a 400
  (before: a 500, or accepted). The reply takes the parent's version, as
  reply_to_comment does.
- tests/test_share_comment_replies.py (real_db).

CHANGELOG: Unreleased > Fixed.
